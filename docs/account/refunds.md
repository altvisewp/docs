# Refunds

AltviseWP offers a 14-day money-back guarantee on all premium product purchases. If you are not satisfied for any reason, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your original purchase date.

## Eligibility

A refund request is eligible if:

- It is submitted within **14 days** of the original purchase date
- The purchase is for a **premium product** (plugins, Divi child themes, or Divi layouts)

Refunds are not available for:

- Requests submitted more than 14 days after the purchase date
- Renewal payments where the license has already been actively used during the renewal period
- Free products

## How to request a refund

1. Email [support@altvisewp.com](mailto:support@altvisewp.com) with the subject line **Refund Request**
2. Include your order number (found in your purchase confirmation email or in **My Account → Orders**)
3. Include a brief reason for the refund — this helps us improve our products, though a reason is not required to receive a refund

We aim to process all refund requests within **2 business days** of receipt. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

## Processing time

Once approved:

- **Credit and debit cards (Stripe)** — refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank
- **PayPal** — refunds typically appear within 3–5 business days

## After a refund

When a refund is processed:

- Your license is deactivated immediately
- You must remove the plugin or theme from all sites where it was installed
- Continued use of the product after a refund is a violation of our [Terms of Service](https://altvisewp.com/terms/)

## Refund policy for renewals

Refunds are not available for renewal payments where the license was actively used during the renewal period. If you did not intend to renew and were charged automatically, contact us at [support@altvisewp.com](mailto:support@altvisewp.com) as soon as possible and we will review your request on a case-by-case basis.

To avoid unexpected renewal charges, cancel auto-renewal before your renewal date under **My Account → Subscriptions**. See [Billing and Renewals](billing-and-renewals.md) for instructions.

## Contact

For refund requests or billing questions, contact us at [support@altvisewp.com](mailto:support@altvisewp.com). We aim to respond to all requests within 2 business days.
