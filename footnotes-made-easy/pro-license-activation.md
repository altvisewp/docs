# License Activation

A valid, active license is required to use Footnotes Made Easy Pro features. Without an active license, the Citations panel is locked and the Library and Gutenberg sidebar are not available.

## Finding your license key

Your license key was emailed to you when you completed your purchase. You can also find it at any time by:

1. Logging in to [altvisewp.com/my-account/](https://altvisewp.com/my-account/)
2. Going to **My Account → My Downloads**
3. Your license key is displayed next to your purchase

## Activating your license

1. Log in to your WordPress admin dashboard
2. Go to **Footnotes → License**
3. Enter your license key in the **License key** field
4. Click **Activate**

If the key is valid, the page will reload and show a green confirmation that your license is active. All Pro features are now unlocked.

## Activation limits

Each license plan allows activation on a set number of sites:

- **Personal** — 1 site
- **Professional** — 3 sites

If you have reached your activation limit and need to activate on a new site, deactivate the license on a site where it is no longer needed before activating on the new one.

## Deactivating your license

To deactivate the license on a site:

1. Go to **Footnotes → License**
2. Click **Deactivate license**
3. Confirm the deactivation

Once deactivated, the activation count for that site is freed and can be used on a different site. Pro features will be locked on the site where the license was deactivated.

## Transferring to a new site

If you are migrating your WordPress site to a new domain or host:

1. Deactivate the license on the old site before migration
2. Complete the site migration
3. Activate the license on the new site

If you have lost access to the old site and cannot deactivate the license there, contact support at [altvisewp.com/support](https://altvisewp.com/support/) and we will manually release the activation.

## License expiry

Annual licenses expire on the anniversary of your purchase date. When a license expires:

- Pro features continue to function — existing footnotes and citations are not affected
- Automatic updates are no longer delivered
- Support access is no longer available

To renew your license, log in to [altvisewp.com/my-account/](https://altvisewp.com/my-account/) and click **Renew** next to your license.

## Multisite license activation

On WordPress multisite networks, the license is activated from the network admin:

1. Log in to your **Network Admin** dashboard
2. Go to **Footnotes → License**
3. Enter your license key and click **Activate**

A network-level activation covers all subsites on the network and counts as one activation regardless of the number of subsites.

## Troubleshooting

### The activation page shows an error

- Check that your license key is entered correctly with no extra spaces
- Confirm your license has not expired in your account dashboard
- Confirm you have not exceeded your activation limit
- If the problem persists, contact [altvisewp.com/support](https://altvisewp.com/support/)

### I cannot see the License page

The License page is only visible when the free Footnotes Made Easy plugin is installed and active. On multisite networks, the License page is only accessible from the network admin.

### My license shows as active but Pro features are locked

Try deactivating and reactivating the license. If the issue persists, deactivate the plugin, delete it, reinstall, and reactivate the license. If the problem continues, contact support.
