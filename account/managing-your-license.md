# Managing Your License

Your AltviseWP license controls access to Pro features, automatic updates, and support. This page covers everything you need to know about managing your license from your account dashboard.

## Accessing your account

Log in at [altvisewp.com/my-account/](https://altvisewp.com/my-account/) using the email address and password you used when purchasing.

If you have forgotten your password, click **Lost your password?** on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

## Viewing your licenses

After logging in, go to **My Account → My Downloads**. You will see a list of all your purchases, each showing:

- Product name
- License key
- License plan (Personal or Professional)
- Activation limit (number of sites allowed)
- Current activations (number of sites currently using this license)
- Expiry date (for annual licenses)
- Status (Active, Expired, or Cancelled)

## Finding your license key

Your license key is displayed on the **My Downloads** page next to each product. It is also included in your original purchase confirmation email.

If you cannot find your purchase confirmation email, log in to your account and retrieve the key from the My Downloads page.

## Activating a license on a site

See the full [License Activation guide](pro-license-activation.md) for step-by-step instructions.

## Deactivating a license

To free up an activation — for example, when moving to a new domain or decommissioning a site:

1. Log in to the WordPress site where the license is active
2. Go to **Footnotes → License**
3. Click **Deactivate license**
4. Confirm the deactivation

The activation is immediately released and can be used on another site. If you no longer have access to the site, contact us at [support@altvisewp.com](mailto:support@altvisewp.com) and we will manually release the activation.

## Viewing active sites

The **My Downloads** page shows the current number of activations for each license (for example, "2 of 3 sites"). To see which specific sites a license is active on, click **View activations** next to the license.

## Transferring a license to a new domain

If your site has moved to a new domain:

1. Deactivate the license on the old domain (via **Footnotes → License → Deactivate**, if you still have access)
2. Activate the license on the new domain (via **Footnotes → License**)

If you have lost access to the old domain, contact support and we will release the activation manually.

## Upgrading your plan

To upgrade from Personal (1 site) to Professional (3 sites):

1. Log in to [altvisewp.com/my-account/](https://altvisewp.com/my-account/)
2. Go to **My Account → My Downloads**
3. Click **Upgrade** next to your license
4. Complete the checkout for the plan difference

Your existing activation(s) are carried over and your license key remains the same.

## Cancelling auto-renewal

Annual licenses renew automatically on their anniversary date. To cancel auto-renewal:

1. Log in to [altvisewp.com/my-account/](https://altvisewp.com/my-account/)
2. Go to **My Account → Subscriptions**
3. Click **Cancel** next to the subscription you want to stop
4. Confirm the cancellation

Cancelling auto-renewal does not immediately deactivate your license — it continues to work until the current expiry date. After that date, the license will expire and Pro features will stop receiving updates.

## Reactivating an expired license

If your license has expired and you want to restore update access and support:

1. Log in to [altvisewp.com/my-account/](https://altvisewp.com/my-account/)
2. Go to **My Account → My Downloads**
3. Click **Renew** next to the expired license
4. Complete the checkout

Your license key remains the same after renewal. No reinstallation is required — simply re-enter your existing license key on the License page or wait for the license status to sync automatically.
